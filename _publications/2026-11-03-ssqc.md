excerpt: "We present an exact static, spherically symmetric regular black-hole solution with a smooth de Sitter-like core. The metric is asymptotically Schwarzschild and real analytic at the center, avoiding the central singularity. The reconstructed magnetic NED source satisfies the Einstein-matter equations, and the characteristic sign conditions hold throughout the exterior. The model is consistent with EHT observations for gamma = 0.35."
